Selected as the LSA Article of the Month for October 2026.

This paper presents the Difficult Airway Society's updated approach to unexpected difficulty with adult tracheal intubation. It draws on a systematic review and expert consensus to address preparation, oxygenation, intubation, rescue strategies, teamwork, and training.

**Authors:** Imran Ahmad and colleagues.

**Publication:** British Journal of Anaesthesia, January 2026; 136(1):283-307. Published online November 7, 2025.
