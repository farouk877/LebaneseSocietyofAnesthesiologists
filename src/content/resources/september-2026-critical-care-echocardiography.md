Selected as the LSA Article of the Month for September 2026.

This review examines the role of critical care echocardiography in haemodynamic assessment, from initial evaluation of shock to repeated monitoring during treatment. It discusses its capabilities and limitations, including when additional continuous monitoring may be needed.

**Authors:** Wincy Ng, Martin Balik, and Michelle S. Chew.

**Publication:** Current Opinion in Critical Care, October 2026; 32(5):545-563. Published online August 12, 2026.
